# Payment transfers

The terms used for sending money to a recipient and tracking the remaining funds.

## Language

**Transfer**:
A payment to a recipient for a specified amount. A successful transfer reduces
the available balance; a rejected transfer does not.

**Available balance**:
The funds available to send through a transfer, expressed in the account's currency.

**Recipient**:
The person and destination account to which a transfer is addressed.

**Transfer approval**:
Permission to submit a specified transfer after the sender authenticates.
It applies to that transfer rather than signing the sender into the app.

**Reviewed transfer**:
The recipient, amount, currency, and note the sender has checked before seeking
transfer approval. Editing these details requires another review.

**Unresolved submission**:
A transfer request whose outcome is not yet known. It may already have completed;
recovery must refer to the same request rather than start another transfer.
